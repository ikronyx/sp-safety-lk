About us

SP Holdings is a leader in workplace safety sector. Our portfolio encompass range of products and services for workplace safety, office management and industrial bearings. Under our work place safety portfolio, we provided reliable and exceptional products that offer greater satisfaction and safety in everyday use personal protective equipment, workplace safety technologies – equipment and systems that enable people to work safely in hazardous environments. Our high-quality PPE portfolio includes protective footwear, respiration protection, head protection equipment, eye and face protection equipment, hearing protection equipment, protective clothing, head and arm protection equipment, and fall arrest systems. SP Holdings Stationaries, is a sole subsidiary geared to provide end to end to office stationary requirements of large to small scale businesses. Under our umbrella we also have SP Holdings Spares, where we provide durable industrial bearings to our customers. Our product line include Bearings, Pullies, Power Transmission Belts, Oil Seals among other spare parts.

Our Vision

To become the market leader in Sri Lanka in the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) market by providing reliable and high quality personal protective equipment, which assures the industry safety standards and prevention and reduction of workplace injuries within Sri Lanka.

Our Mission

To passionately deliver outstanding products and services to enrich lives of our customers with the focus of promoting workplace health and safety for all individuals who work within hazardous environments.
